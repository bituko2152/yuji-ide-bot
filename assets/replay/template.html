Livery per driver

Easiest: put 1.png … 10.png in the liveries folder next to this page and set who drives which in liveries/config.js. Images are top-down cars with the nose pointing right and a transparent background (around 109 × 45 px). Uploads and choices made here override the folder, in this browser only.

Positions are recorded at 60 per second from the lobby traffic and synced to the race clock. The order and intervals are worked out from each car's progress around the lap, so they can differ by a few tenths from the in-game timing screen.
